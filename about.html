How I work

01
Craft first, process second.

Good design comes from taste, curiosity, and iteration — not frameworks. I use process to protect space for craft, not to replace it.

02
Design and code are the same conversation.

I prototype in the browser, write my own CSS, and use AI tools to close the gap between what I imagine and what ships. The closer I stay to the material, the better the decisions.

03
Leadership means raising the floor.

I care more about making a team 30% better than being the best individual contributor. Strong feedback culture, clear principles, shared ownership.
