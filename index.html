The hardest problem in AI product design isn't the interface. It's the moment a user decides whether to trust the output — and acts on it.

I've been designing for that moment since before it was called an AI problem. At IG Group it was a trading decision. At Deel it's a workforce one. The stakes change. The design problem doesn't.
